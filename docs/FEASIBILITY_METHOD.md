# Feasibility Method

Proposed evidence dimensions:

- market opportunity
- location suitability
- observed competition signal
- demand proxy
- connectivity/context
- capital fit
- operating economics
- scheme fit
- evidence confidence

The score is a **decision-support index**, not a probability of business success. All weights are versioned. Inputs retain provenance. Missing evidence must reduce confidence and should not silently become a zero.

For any future statistical model, keep the model disabled until a labelled training/validation dataset and calibration evidence exist.
