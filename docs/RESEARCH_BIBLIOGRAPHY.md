## Academic / technical research

### Rural entrepreneurship / spatial context
- Korsgaard, S., Bosworth, G., Gaddefors, J. (2026). “Entrepreneurship for the rural? A reflection on rural entrepreneurship in a time of multi-crisis.” Small Business Economics. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11187-026-01184-w
  YUKTI use: motivates spatial-context framing and cautions against simplistic rural/urban generalization.
- Yang, X., Li, W., Gao, Z. (2025). “Spatial attribution and policy design of rural entrepreneurship: Evidence from Anhui Province in China.” PLOS ONE 20(9), e0331419. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331419
  YUKTI use: informs use of spatial clustering/geographic variables as contextual evidence, not as proof of business success.
- Garg, S., Gupta, S., Mallick, S. (2026). “Does social identity constrain rural entrepreneurship? Evidence on the role of financial inclusion.” Journal of Development Economics, 179, 103669. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jdeveco.2025.103669
  YUKTI use: supports the relevance of access-to-finance proximity/context for rural entrepreneurship analysis.

### Population modelling / geospatial
- Wardrop, N. A. et al. (2018). “Spatially disaggregated population estimates in the absence of national population and housing census data.” PNAS 115(14):3529–3537. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1715305115
  YUKTI use: supports the conceptual distinction between census and modelled disaggregated population.
- Bondarenko, M. et al. (2020). “Census/projection-disaggregated gridded population datasets for 189 countries in 2020 using Built-Settlement Growth Model (BSGM) outputs.” DOI: https://doi.org/10.5258/SOTON/WP00684
  YUKTI use: explains why spatial population surfaces are estimates derived from census/projections and built-settlement covariates.

### Retail catchment / spatial competition
- “Buy online collect in-store: exploring grocery click&collect using a national case study” (2018), International Journal of Retail & Distribution Management. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1108/IJRDM-01-2018-0025
  YUKTI use: supports catchment thinking and rural/urban catchment differences.
- Nilsson, J., Smirnov (2017). “Clustering vs. relative location: Measuring spatial interaction between retail outlets.” Papers in Regional Science. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/pirs.12219
  YUKTI use: motivates spatial competition signals beyond raw counts.
- Davis, P. (2006). “Spatial competition in retail markets: movie theaters.” RAND Journal of Economics 37(4):964–982. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1756-2171.2006.tb00066.x
  YUKTI use: supports the idea that location and consumer proximity influence retail demand.

### Uncertainty / calibration
- 2026 benchmark article: “Calibration, Architecture, and Distribution Shift in Predictive Uncertainty Estimation.” Machine Learning and Knowledge Extraction 8(7):179. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/make8070179
  YUKTI use: reinforces that uncertainty quality requires explicit evaluation and that an uncalibrated heuristic should not be presented as a probability.

## Model-development rule

No paper is used as a claim that YUKTI itself has validated performance. Literature informs method selection and limitations; YUKTI must separately validate any predictive model on its own labelled data.
